How Pyrosite Works

What this tracks

Pyrosite is a real-time countdown for the Executive Hangar LED cycle inside Star Citizen's Pyro contested zone (PYAM-EXHANG-0-1). It tells you, at a glance, which sub-phase the doors are in, when the next transition will happen, and whether it's safe to insert a compboard.

How it stays in sync

The math runs in your browser, anchored to one fixed timestamp. Because Date.now() is UTC on every device, two players opening this from opposite continents see the exact same countdown.

If the timer feels off

CIG sometimes resets the cycle in a patch or during a server maintenance. Open the config panel above, drag the slider to the current phase you see in-game, hit Apply, the state you set will persist through browser closes and refreshes.

Three phases

  • Charging. Red, 120 minutes. Door is locked, don't waste a compboard.
  • Active. Green, 60 minutes. Door opens, compboards work.
  • Reset. Black, 5 minutes. Death zone, stay clear.
